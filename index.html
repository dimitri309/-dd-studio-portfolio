01
Coffee Shop

Brew & Bloom

Immersive scroll-snap gallery with real photography

View Project →
HTML · CSS · JS · AI
Brief

A coffee shop site that feels as carefully crafted as the coffee itself. Every detail considered, nothing wasted.

Approach

Full-screen scroll-snap panels — each section fills the viewport and locks into place as you move through it. Real photography throughout, because a coffee shop sells an atmosphere as much as a drink.

What makes it different

The menu lives in a horizontal filmstrip you drag through, each drink with its own photo and description. Maximum impact, minimum space. Grain texture overlay, parallax hero, split-text headline reveal, smooth momentum on every interaction.

Outcome

This is a demo. But if a coffee shop owner knows what they want — their photos, their menu, their story — this site is already most of the way there.

02
Fine Dining

Osteria Lina

Cinematic horizontal scroll with magnetic interactions

View Project →
HTML · CSS · JS · AI
Brief

Build a restaurant site that feels as premium as the dining experience itself. No templates, no generic layouts.

Approach

Instead of the standard vertical scroll every restaurant site uses, we built a full horizontal journey — six cinematic chapters the user moves through left to right, each with its own distinct layout and personality. Less like a website, more like turning pages of an editorial magazine.

What makes it different

A custom cursor with a trailing ring, magnetic buttons that physically follow your cursor, 3D-tilt gallery photos, smooth inertia scrolling, and a chapter-by-chapter parallax system. Every interaction signals one thing: quality.

Outcome

A site that makes a restaurant owner look at their current Instagram page and immediately understand what they're missing.

03
Gym & Fitness

FORGE Athletic

Sticky split-screen with scrollytelling image swap

View Project →
HTML · CSS · JS · AI
Brief

Gyms either have no website or a boring one. Build something that matches the energy of actually training.

Approach

FORGE uses a sticky split-screen — the left half frozen on a full-height gym photo that swaps as you scroll, the right side moving through sections. A format you see on premium agency sites. Not on gym websites. Until now.

What makes it different

Three membership tiers for instant comparison, trainer profiles so clients pick their coach before walking in, a contact form with a goal dropdown. The left-side photo changes automatically as you scroll — the site shows you what you're about to read.

Outcome

A site that makes someone feel like they're already a member before they've signed up.

04
Dental Clinic

Lumio Dental

Multi-step booking form with side navigation

View Project →
HTML · CSS · JS · AI
Brief

Most dental sites look clinical and cold. Build one that feels premium and trustworthy without losing professionalism.

Approach

A full 3-step appointment booking system — choose service, pick date and time slot, confirm details. Each step transitions with smooth animation. A numbered side navigation guides patients through seven sections without ever feeling lost.

What makes it different

The interactions do the trust-building. A calm cursor, soft scroll reveals, an accordion service explorer that explains each treatment before a patient picks up the phone. When a patient understands the service, they book with confidence.

Outcome

A dental site that works as hard as the clinic — answering questions, reducing anxiety, converting visitors into booked appointments before they even call.
