services / what you get

Everything, handled

One person does the design, the code, the hosting and the handoff. Nothing gets lost between departments — there are none.

svc/01

Custom design

Designed from zero around your business — your room, your food, your tone. If a layout could belong to your competitor, it doesn't ship.

svc/02

Modern build

Hand-built with Next.js, React, Tailwind, GSAP and Three.js where it earns its place. Fast on cheap phones, sharp on big screens.

svc/03

Hosting, free

Domain, hosting and deployment set up for you at no extra cost. Your site goes live and stays live — you never touch a server.

svc/04

Yours to edit

A simple handoff so you change menus, prices and photos yourself — no developer on speed dial, no invoice for fixing a typo.

about / the studio

One person. On purpose.

I'm Dima. DD Studio is just me — and that's exactly why it works.

Every project here was designed and built by the same pair of hands. When you hire DD Studio you talk to the person who will actually move the pixels and write the code — not an account manager translating your words to a team you'll never meet.

I work from Tbilisi and build mostly for local businesses: cafés, restaurants, gyms, clinics. Places whose sites are usually an afterthought — a template with the logo swapped. I think a corner coffee shop deserves the same design attention as a startup with funding. That belief is the studio.

Each build starts from what makes that business different, which is why no two projects above share a layout, a typeface system, or a scroll. If you want a website that looks like everyone else's, I'm the wrong person. Happily.
